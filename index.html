← HomeResearch

Mathematical models. Computational questions.

My research connects computational fracture mechanics and scientific machine learning, with applications to bone fracture, differential equations, and image reconstruction.

DOCTORAL RESEARCH

How does bone fracture under impact?

I develop peridynamic simulations of crack initiation and propagation in bone under blunt force impact, working with Dr. Victoria Howle and collaborators in forensic anthropology.

My computational workflow combines bone geometry and impact information in Peridigm. Preliminary simulations capture some broad trends in damage, while reproducing individual fracture patterns remains an active research challenge. Current work focuses on specimen-specific geometry and comparison with physical experiments.

SCIENTIFIC MACHINE LEARNING

Neural networks for differential equations

My published work investigates feedforward neural-network methods for nonlinear and singular ordinary differential equations. I have also contributed code and manuscript writing to physics-informed Kolmogorov–Arnold network studies of the Thomas–Fermi equation and higher-order boundary value problems.

COLLABORATIVE RESEARCH

Diffusion-based image super-resolution

Through the WiSDM collaboration, I contributed code for color-image experiments in a study of perceptually regularized diffusion models for image super-resolution.

Read the preprint ↗

Looking ahead

I am interested in specimen-specific fracture modeling, hybrid numerical and machine-learning solvers, and reliable learning-based methods for simulation and inverse problems.

A path through mathematics and computing.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics at Texas Tech University, advised by Dr. Victoria Howle. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in August 2027.

My academic path began with a bachelor’s degree in civil engineering and led to graduate study in applied and computational mathematics. I am interested in using mathematical models and computational tools to understand scientific problems.

Alongside research, I teach undergraduate mathematics and value opportunities to collaborate across disciplines. I served as Treasurer of the SIAM–TTU Student Chapter from July 2024 to July 2025.
